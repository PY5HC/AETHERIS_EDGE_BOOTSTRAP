## Admission interpretation

The evidence class is `LOCAL_AI_EXPERIMENTAL`. The model is not promoted to
`LOCAL_AI_SMALL` or `LOCAL_AI_MEDIUM` without coexistence and workload
measurements. A future scheduler must reserve headroom for Knowledge
ingestion/query, embeddings, reranking, GraphRAG, OCR/document processing,
RF realtime DSP, RF feature extraction, and RF ML inference. Realtime RF
workloads take precedence; Local AI work must yield or be rejected when the
measured resource budget cannot preserve those workloads.

No swap was enabled, no stress soak was run, and no production concurrency
limit is claimed by this record. Formal comparison and admission thresholds
remain G4.5 follow-on evidence.
